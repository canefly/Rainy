**Quick way (no assistant):** open your script, then use **Export, Premiere XML**. You get the A-roll cards laid flat on a timeline. No voiceover, no matching, nothing smart. See [Export Premiere XML on its own](guide:export-xml).

**Automatic way (this part):** Rainy lines up your voiceover with the script, cuts out all the silence, and places the A-roll where the script calls for it. For this you need an AI assistant connected to Rainy. Do these five steps in order:

1. [Open the Footage Manager](guide:footage-manager)
2. [Link your footage](guide:link-footage)
3. [Transcribe the voiceover](guide:transcribe)
4. [Check the voiceover](guide:check-voiceover) (optional, but quick)
5. [Connect an AI assistant](guide:connect-ai), then ask it to build the timeline
