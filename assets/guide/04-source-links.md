Open them with the link icon in the left bar. Think of it as a smart checklist: it lists every link found in your script and tells you about each one.

- **Tick** a link when you have handled it.
- **Info** shows what the link says (its title and details), and whether its footage is linked on this computer.
- You **cannot link footage here.** This window only tells you the state. If a link shows its footage is **missing**, go back to the Footage Manager and fix it there: [Link your footage](guide:link-footage).

**Link details not showing?** For private Google Drive links, Rainy needs permission to read them. Set it up in [Settings, Cookies & Accounts](app:settings/cookies) by importing your Firefox cookies, using a Firefox that is signed in to the Google account that can open those files.
